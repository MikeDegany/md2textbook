# 🤖 CoRL 2026 Workshops: Complete Comparison

> **Conference:** CoRL 2026 · Austin, Texas · main conference Nov 9–11 · **all workshops Thursday, Nov 12, 2026** (JW Marriott Austin)
> **Compiled:** Oct 1, 2026 from each workshop's **own website** (not from the CoRL index page)
> **Coverage:** 31 of 32 workshops read in full. **SPIN** could not be read (its Google Site redirects to a sign-in wall).

**Conventions**
- Deadlines are as written on each workshop's site. **AoE** = Anywhere on Earth (UTC−12). "Today" = Oct 1, 2026.
- ⚠️ = already passed, closing today, or closing within a week.
- "Not posted" means the workshop's own site does not say. I did not fill any gap with a guess.
- All times are Austin local time (CST).
